Synopsis

Yhwach's Quincy forces have already crushed much of the Soul Society, and now Yhwach himself is nearly unstoppable. His power, The Almighty, lets him see and rewrite the future, and he has absorbed the powers of those around him, including Ichigo's Quincy blood and Soul King-related abilities. The Gotei 13 and Ichigo's allies stage a desperate last stand. Kyoraku, Ukitake's legacy, Byakuya, Renji, and others fight to buy time, while Ichigo, still recovering from his own transformation and the revelation of his heritage, races to confront Yhwach. The final clash pits Ichigo against Yhwach in the Soul King's palace, with Ichigo's Bankai and Hollow-Quincy-Shinigami hybrid power finally matching Yhwach's. Ichigo breaks Yhwach's grip on fate, and Yhwach is ultimately defeated, ending the war. In the aftermath, the Soul Society is left battered but standing, Ichigo returns to the human world with a clearer sense of who he is, and the series closes on a note of hard-won peace and the cost of the war.
