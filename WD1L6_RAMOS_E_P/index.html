How I approach design

Our current technology is a means to an end. Every tiny optimization is a step towards convenience.
Start with the person using it
Before even any ideation, I ask: who will use this? What are they trying to finish?
Keep it simple
Just like machinery, less moving parts means less things to go wrong.
Make it work for everyone
Keyboard navigation, readable contrast, and fast loading times are a must.
Continuous maintenance needs user connections
I believe users are the best form of a roadmap in terms of things to improve, add, or omit.
