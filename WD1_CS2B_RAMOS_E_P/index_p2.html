A Secret Within

Our story begins with a girl named Maya.
She was caught in the rain on the way home.
She ran to the public park where there was shelter, and sat on one of the benches
There, Maya would find a wet notebook. She took it out of curiosity.
Upon opening it, she discovered drawings of places around town.
But there was one sketch at the end of the notebook which was not familiar.
It came with a small sketch of a map, so out of curiosity
she followed.

Maya would find herself in front of the public museum,
where the map would take her to a path hidden behind thick foliage.
She followed the path.
Pushing through another thick bush, she would stumble into a beautiful, but abandoned garden
She sat on the grass, taking in the sight and the small adventure she just experienced.
But night was fast approaching, and she had to go home. So she headed out.
Before she would head home though, she left that notebook back in its place, hoping to meet more curious souls when she would eventually visit once more.

The End
